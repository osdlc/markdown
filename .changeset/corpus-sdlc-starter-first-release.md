The first release of `@osdlc/corpus-sdlc-starter`: a starter knowledge base for an sdlc planning
corpus, with two draft principles. Its types come from `@osdlc/corpus-profile-sdlc`. A repo names
it under `extends` in its corpus's root file, and cites its records by id. It needs
`@osdlc/corpus-schema` 0.3.0 or later. Licence `MIT OR Apache-2.0`.
